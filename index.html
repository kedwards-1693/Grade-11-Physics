How to use these lessons

  1. Open the lesson and read the part, then try the activity or example before you reveal the answer.
  2. Do the knowledge check. Read the feedback on wrong answers, because it explains what went wrong.
  3. Do the matching Google Classroom practice the lesson points you to.
  4. Rate yourself at the end and copy your results summary to share.

Work for about an hour a day. If you get stuck for more than ten minutes, mark the question and move on.

The routine for every problem

Most lost marks in physics come from jumping straight to an equation. Use the same steps every time:

Sketch it, and choose which direction is positive.

Given: list every number with its unit, including hidden ones (“from rest” means starting velocity 0).

Unknown: say what the question asks for.

Equation: pick the one that has what you know and what you want.

Solve: rearrange first, then put in numbers. Round at the end.

Sense-check: units, sign, and does the size make sense?
